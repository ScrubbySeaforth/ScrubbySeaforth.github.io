Child Safety Comes First

Scrubby is a child-owned local business. Because of this, the safety and wellbeing of the children involved is our highest priority. Every customer enquiry is handled by an adult first. The adult responsible for Scrubby reviews the enquiry and decides whether the customer and job are safe and appropriate before anything is arranged.

📍 Local Jobs Only

We only accept jobs within our local area so that the children are working close to home.

🚫 No Home Entry

Scrubby services are carried out outside. Children will never be expected to enter a customer's home.

👨‍👧 Adult Screening

Every enquiry goes through an adult first. No customer is automatically accepted. The adult responsible for Scrubby decides whether the person, location and job are safe and appropriate.

🔒 Privacy Protected

We do not publish children's names, ages, home address, personal contact details, school information or private schedules.

☀️ Daylight Working

Jobs are carried out during daylight hours. We do not arrange late-night appointments.

✋ Right To Leave

If anything feels unsafe, uncomfortable or inappropriate, the job will not continue and the children can leave.

📧 Adult-Managed Contact

Customer communication is handled through the official Scrubby email and reviewed by an adult before a job is accepted.

🚷 No Private Contact

Customers should not attempt to contact, follow or arrange separate work with the children outside of the official Scrubby booking process.

Please respect the boundaries of our child-owned business. The safety, privacy and wellbeing of the children involved will always come before accepting a job.

📧 When you email Scrubby
